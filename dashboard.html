Bank of Ghana Regulation

Why is this verification required?

Under the Bank of Ghana's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Regulations, all financial institutions must verify the identity of customers for transactions exceeding $40,000.

What happens if I don't verify?

Your payout may be held by the Bank of Ghana until verification is complete. This is a regulatory requirement that applies to all financial institutions in Ghana.

What information is needed?

• Tax Identification Number
• Government-issued ID
• Biometric verification (in-person at our Accra office)

Is my information secure?

Yes. All information is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with data protection regulations.
